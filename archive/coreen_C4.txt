어디에서 왔어요?	Where are you from?	가: 어디에서 왔어요? 나: 프랑스에서 왔어요.<br>A: Where are you from? B: I'm from France.	Literally "where did you come from". The everyday way to ask someone's origin.	cours::C4 교재::L1 표현
-에서 왔어요	I came from ~	저는 리옹에서 왔어요.<br>I'm from Lyon.	에서 marks the origin point; 왔어요 is the past of 오다. Pronounced [와써요].	cours::C4 교재::L1 문법
오다	to come	친구가 한국에 와요.<br>My friend is coming to Korea.	Present 와요, past 왔어요. The 오+아 contraction gives 와.	cours::C4 교재::L1 동사
-씨	Mr / Ms (after a name)	투이 씨, 어디에서 왔어요?<br>Thuy, where are you from?	Attaches to a given name or full name — never to a family name alone, and never to yourself.	cours::C4 교재::L1 표현
이에요/예요	to be (polite ending)	학생이에요. / 의사예요.<br>I'm a student. / I'm a doctor.	이에요 after a consonant, 예요 after a vowel. Same form for statements and questions — only the intonation changes.	cours::C4 교재::L1 문법
저	I (humble)	저는 대학생이에요.<br>I'm a university student.	Humble form of 나. Use 저 with anyone you'd address politely.	cours::C4 교재::L1 대명사
제	my (humble)	제 이름은 막서스예요.<br>My name is Maxence.	Contraction of 저의. Casual equivalent: 내 (from 나의).	cours::C4 교재::L1 대명사
우리	our / my	우리 언니예요.<br>She's my older sister.	Korean uses 우리 where French would use "mon" for family, school or country — 우리 집, 우리 나라.	cours::C4 교재::L1 대명사
누구	who	이 사람은 누구예요?<br>Who is this person?	누구 + 예요 → 누구예요. As a subject it contracts: 누가.	cours::C4 교재::L1 대명사
사람	person	저 사람은 제 친구예요.<br>That person is my friend.	Also forms nationalities: 한국 사람, 프랑스 사람.	cours::C4 교재::L1 명사
언니	older sister (of a woman)	우리 언니예요.<br>She's my older sister.	Only a female speaker says 언니. A male speaker says 누나.	cours::C4 교재::L1 가족
누나	older sister (of a man)	누나가 두 명 있어요.<br>I have two older sisters.	Used by a male speaker only — this is the one you'd use.	cours::C4 교재::L1 가족
오빠	older brother (of a woman)	오빠는 회사원이에요.<br>My older brother is an office worker.	Female speaker only. Also used for an older boyfriend or a male friend.	cours::C4 교재::L1 가족
형	older brother (of a man)	이 사람은 우리 형이에요.<br>This is my older brother.	Male speaker only — the one that applies to you.	cours::C4 교재::L1 가족
