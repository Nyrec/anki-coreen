-으면서/-면서	while doing	음악을 들으면서 요리해요.<br>I cook while listening to music.	Both actions by the same subject. Consonant → -으면서, vowel or ㄹ → -면서 (놀면서). Irregulars: 듣다 → 들으면서, 걷다 → 걸으면서, 줍다 → 주우면서.	cours::J05 교재::MKG-UB7 문법
제가 살게요	It's on me, I'll pay	점심은 제가 살게요.<br>Lunch is on me.	사다 + -ㄹ게요. The standard way to offer to treat someone.	cours::J05 교재::MKG-UB7 표현
먼저 갈게요	I'll head off first	죄송하지만, 저는 먼저 갈게요.<br>Sorry, but I'll get going.	Said when leaving before others. At work: 먼저 퇴근할게요.	cours::J05 교재::MKG-UB7 표현
잠시만요, 지나갈게요	Excuse me, coming through	잠시만요, 지나갈게요.<br>Excuse me, let me through.	지나가다 = to pass by. Heard constantly in crowded buses and markets.	cours::J05 교재::MKG-UB7 표현
