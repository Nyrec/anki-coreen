안녕하세요.	Hello	안녕하세요, 처음 뵙겠습니다.<br>Hello, pleased to meet you.	Works any time of day. More formal: 안녕하십니까.	cours::C3 인사
안녕히 가세요.	Goodbye (to the person leaving)	안녕히 가세요, 조심히 들어가세요.<br>Goodbye, get home safely.	Said when you stay and the other person goes. Literally "go in peace".	cours::C3 인사
안녕히 계세요.	Goodbye (to the person staying)	안녕히 계세요, 내일 봬요.<br>Goodbye, see you tomorrow.	Said when you are the one leaving. Literally "stay in peace".	cours::C3 인사
네 / 아니요	Yes / No	네, 맞아요. / 아니요, 괜찮아요.<br>Yes, that's right. / No, I'm fine.	네 also works as a neutral "I see" or acknowledgement, not only as "yes".	cours::C3 인사
알았어요.	Okay / Got it	네, 알았어요.<br>Okay, got it.	Pronounced [아라써요] by liaison. Casual with peers; use 알겠습니다 with superiors.	cours::C3 인사 발음
여보세요?	Hello? (on the phone)	여보세요? 누구세요?<br>Hello? Who's calling?	Phone only — never used face to face.	cours::C3 인사
죄송합니다.	I'm sorry	늦어서 죄송합니다.<br>I'm sorry for being late.	More formal than 미안합니다.	cours::C3 인사
실례합니다.	Excuse me	실례합니다, 길 좀 물을게요.<br>Excuse me, may I ask for directions?	For getting attention or interrupting, not for apologising.	cours::C3 인사
감사합니다.	Thank you	도와주셔서 감사합니다.<br>Thank you for helping me.	고맙습니다 is the native-Korean equivalent, slightly warmer.	cours::C3 인사
몰라요.	I don't know	저도 잘 몰라요.<br>I don't really know either.	From 모르다, a 르-irregular verb.	cours::C3 인사
알아요.	I know	그 사람을 알아요.<br>I know that person.	Pronounced [아라요] by liaison.	cours::C3 인사 발음
좋아요.	Sounds good / Sure	좋아요, 그렇게 해요.<br>Sounds good, let's do it.	Pronounced [조아요]: a final ㅎ is usually dropped before a vowel. Formal form 좋습니다 → [조씀니다].	cours::C3 인사 발음
잘 먹겠습니다.	Thanks for the meal (before eating)	잘 먹겠습니다!<br>Thanks for the meal, I'll enjoy it!	Pronounced [잘 머께씀니다]: ㄱ+ㄱ tenses the following consonant, and ㅂ before ㄴ becomes [ㅁ]. Said to whoever is treating you.	cours::C3 인사 발음
잘 먹었습니다.	Thanks for the meal (after eating)	잘 먹었습니다, 정말 맛있었어요.<br>Thanks for the meal, it was really good.	Pronounced [잘 머거씀니다]. Same phrase in the past tense — the pair bookends the meal.	cours::C3 인사 발음
괜찮아요.	It's okay	괜찮아요, 신경 쓰지 마세요.<br>It's fine, don't worry about it.	Pronounced [괜차나요], ㅎ dropped. Covers "I'm fine", "no thanks" and "no problem".	cours::C3 인사 발음
Why is 좋아요 pronounced [조아요]?	A final ㅎ is usually dropped before a vowel	좋아요 → [조아요], 괜찮아요 → [괜차나요], 싫어요 → [시러요]	Rule from this lesson: ㅎ as a 받침 is rarely pronounced. Before ㄱ/ㄷ/ㅈ it instead aspirates them: 좋고 → [조코].	cours::C3 발음규칙
What happens when identical or similar consonants meet?	The following consonant becomes tense	먹겠습니다 → [머께씀니다], 학교 → [학꾜], 입국 → [입꾹]	Rule from this lesson: after a stop 받침 (ㄱ, ㄷ, ㅂ), a following plain consonant is realized as its tense counterpart.	cours::C3 발음규칙
