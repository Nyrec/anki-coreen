제때	on time, at the right time<br><i>à temps, au bon moment</i>	밥을 제때 먹어야 건강해요.<br>You have to eat at the proper times to stay healthy.<br><i>Il faut manger à heures régulières pour être en bonne santé.</i>	[jettae] Used without particle as an adverb, or with 에 (제때에). Common with deadlines: 보고서를 제때 제출하다. Pronounced [제때].	quotidien::2026-11-12 부사 niveau::B1
갈등	conflict, tension<br><i>conflit, tension</i>	팀원들 사이에 갈등이 생겼어요.<br>A conflict arose between the team members.<br><i>Un conflit est apparu entre les membres de l'équipe.</i>	[galdeung] Hanja 葛藤 (arrowroot + wisteria vines tangling). Common collocations: 갈등이 생기다, 갈등을 해결하다, 세대 갈등 (generational conflict).	quotidien::2026-11-12 명사 niveau::B1
