바래다주다	to walk/see someone home, to see someone off<br><i>raccompagner (qqn chez lui / jusqu'à un endroit)</i>	늦었으니까 집까지 바래다줄게요.<br>It's late, so I'll walk you home.<br><i>Il est tard, je vais te raccompagner jusque chez toi.</i>	[baraedajuda] Built from 바래다 (to see off) + -아/어 주다 (doing something for someone). Usually with 까지: 역까지 바래다주다. Don't confuse with 바라다 (to hope).	quotidien::2026-11-20 동사 niveau::B1
거절하다	to refuse, to turn down<br><i>refuser, décliner</i>	회식 초대를 정중하게 거절했어요.<br>I politely turned down the invitation to the team dinner.<br><i>J'ai poliment décliné l'invitation au dîner d'équipe.</i>	[geojeolhada] Hanja 拒絶. Takes an object with 을/를 (제안을 거절하다). Noun 거절 = refusal; 거절당하다 = to be rejected. Opposite: 받아들이다 / 수락하다.	quotidien::2026-11-20 동사 niveau::B1
촉박하다	tight, pressing (time/deadline)<br><i>serré, pressant (délai)</i>	마감 시간이 너무 촉박해요.<br>The deadline is way too tight.<br><i>Le délai est vraiment trop serré.</i>	[chokbakhada] Hanja 促迫. Used almost only with time: 시간이/일정이 촉박하다. A more casual near-synonym is 빠듯하다.	quotidien::2026-11-20 형용사 niveau::B2
